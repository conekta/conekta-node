# ApplePaySessionResponse

Apple Pay merchant session returned by Apple. Pass this object as-is to `session.completeMerchantValidation` in the browser. 

## Properties

Name | Type | Description | Notes
------------ | ------------- | ------------- | -------------
**epochTimestamp** | **number** | Date and time, in milliseconds since epoch, when the session was created. | [default to undefined]
**expiresAt** | **number** | Date and time, in milliseconds since epoch, when the session expires. | [default to undefined]
**merchantSessionIdentifier** | **string** | Unique identifier of the merchant session generated by Apple. | [default to undefined]
**nonce** | **string** | Single-use value used by Apple to verify the session. | [default to undefined]
**merchantIdentifier** | **string** | Hashed Apple Pay merchant identifier the session was validated with. | [default to undefined]
**domainName** | **string** | Domain the session was validated for. | [default to undefined]
**displayName** | **string** | Merchant name shown in the Apple Pay sheet. | [default to undefined]
**signature** | **string** | Apple signature of the session, in hexadecimal format. | [default to undefined]
**operationalAnalyticsIdentifier** | **string** | Identifier used by Apple for analytics purposes. | [optional] [default to undefined]
**retries** | **number** | Number of times the session validation has been retried. | [optional] [default to undefined]
**pspId** | **string** | Payment service provider identifier assigned by Apple. | [optional] [default to undefined]
